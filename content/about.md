I'm a platform engineer. Most of my working hours go into the parts of a system that nobody notices until they're slow: the kernel's network stack, the path a packet takes through a datacenter fabric, and the storage engine underneath a database. I like problems where the answer starts with a back-of-the-envelope number and ends with a flame graph.

On the side, I do [mechanistic interpretability](/research/) research. I want to know what a language model is actually computing, in the same way I'd want to know what a kernel is actually doing when a p99 spikes. The research log is where I keep work in progress, mistakes included.
