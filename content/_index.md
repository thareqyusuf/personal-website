I'm a platform engineer, working remotely for teams in Estonia, Singapore and Denmark. Most of my hours go into debugging Kubernetes infrastructure: the network datapath (Cilium, eBPF, or plain `tc` flower), runtime security (Falco, Tetragon), tracing with OpenTelemetry, GPU scheduling for research clusters, and a fast-reset sandbox (Firecracker microVMs). My interest in computer science started with opening up kernel traces to understand how a program works (since computer is a complex, yet deterministic system).

That is the contrast with my current interest, mechanistic interpretability, even with a model's internals open in front of us, we still can't model how it behaves. My focus is AI safety, specifically AI control, understanding adversarial model behaviour along a multi-level attack trajectory.
